# Product Direction v2

## Decision

5SOffice AI Suite will no longer be managed as eight parallel standalone AI applications. It will become a **shared AI Agent capability platform** delivering a focused portfolio of agents for 5SOffice tenants and SMEs.

## Why the direction changed

The original module model established useful domain ideas, but parallel implementation would create excessive integration, data, governance and go-to-market complexity. A shared platform with focused agents provides:

- one customer workspace;
- one orchestration and approval model;
- reusable knowledge, tools and audit services;
- faster delivery of complete workflows;
- clearer packaging and value for SME customers;
- a stronger evidence base for AI governance and AIMS work.

## Product statement

> 5SOffice AI Workspace provides governed AI agents and digital services that help SME tenants complete office, administrative, compliance and growth work through one conversational and workflow interface.

## Strategic relationship

| Component | Primary responsibility |
|---|---|
| 5SOffice Webapp | Discovery, customer portal, tenant experience and entry channel |
| Special AI Customer Service Cockpit | Orchestration, guardrails, human-in-the-loop, administration and escalation |
| 5SOffice AI Suite | Agent definitions, domain workflows, reusable tools and SME capabilities |
| Voucher & AI Credit Engine | Entitlements, quotas, redemptions and usage rights |
| Optional Web3 Adapter | On-chain representation or proof for selected campaigns |
| HQC AI / AIMS assets | Governance methods, evidence design and expert services |

## Priorities

1. Tenant Agent.
2. Administrative Agent.
3. Compliance Agent.
4. Growth Agent.
5. Management Agent.

## Product principles

1. **Workflow before content generation.** Agents should complete a useful process, not merely produce text.
2. **Human confirmation before consequential action.** Booking, redemption, external communication and professional conclusions require appropriate confirmation or approval.
3. **Tenant isolation by design.** Data, retrieval, tools, logs and configuration must be tenant-scoped.
4. **Evidence by design.** Significant actions create traceable records containing user intent, policy decision, approval, tool result and final status.
5. **Progressive integration.** Start with 5SOffice-owned data and APIs before connecting third-party SME systems.
6. **Provider neutrality.** Business workflows must not depend on a single model vendor.
