# Projects Overview

The 5SOffice AI Suite consists of modular AI SaaS capabilities designed for SMEs.

## Modules

### 1. AI Meeting Scheduler & Optimizer
Automates scheduling and room booking with calendar integrations.

### 2. AI Document Management & Automation
OCR-based document classification and workflow automation.

### 3. AI HR Assistant
Supports recruitment screening and onboarding workflows.

### 4. AI Office Analytics & Predictive Maintenance
Provides usage insights and predictive maintenance recommendations.

### 5. AI Content & Marketing Generator
Generates marketing content and supports campaign experimentation.

### 6. AI Customer Support Chatbot
Chat and voice AI for internal and tenant customer support.

### 7. AI Expense Tracker & Financial Insights
Tracks expenses and provides budget forecasting.

### 8. AI Legal Compliance Tracker & Advisor
Monitors regulatory changes, explains impact, and provides actionable checklists.

## Platform characteristics

- Multi-tenant isolation
- Portal-based access
- Explainable AI outputs
- Human oversight for high-impact domains
- ISO/IEC 42001 aligned governance
