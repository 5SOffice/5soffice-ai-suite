# Pricing & Packaging (Hypothesis v1)

This document outlines initial packaging hypotheses for 5SOffice AI Suite.  
It will be revised after the pilot based on usage and willingness-to-pay.

## Packaging principles
- Align plans with SME maturity and role needs
- Keep entry plan simple (fast adoption)
- Charge more for high-impact and high-touch features (Legal, HR, voice support)
- Maintain strict tenant isolation and baseline governance across all plans

## Proposed plans (initial)

### Plan A — Starter
Target: small teams, early-stage SMEs  
Includes:
- Basic AI meeting scheduling (Level 1)
- Limited content templates
- Basic support chatbot (text only)
Limits:
- Usage caps
- No advanced legal alerts

### Plan B — Growth
Target: SMEs running active sales/ops, needing automation  
Includes:
- Document automation (OCR workflows)
- Content generator with campaign templates
- Support chatbot with routing
- Expense tracking basic insights
Limits:
- Some high-impact features require manual confirmation (Level 2 rules apply)

### Plan C — Compliance+
Target: SMEs sensitive to legal/tax/HR compliance  
Includes:
- Legal compliance tracker (alerts + explainable checklists)
- Source citations and effective dates (Tier 1)
- Compliance-focused dashboard
- Escalation workflow to human support (ticketing)
Notes:
- Legal outputs always include transparency and limitation statements

### Plan D — Enterprise / Custom
Target: larger tenants, higher assurance  
Includes:
- Advanced RBAC
- Custom integrations
- Enhanced audit reporting
- Dedicated support / SLA
- Optional governance extensions (AIMS maturity, audits)

## Pricing model options (to validate)
- Per tenant / per month (base)
- Add-ons: Voice, Legal alerts volume, OCR volume, extra seats
- Overages: usage-based for heavy OCR/voice

## Next steps
- Define pilot pricing (introductory)
- Collect usage metrics and retention signals
- Adjust plan boundaries and add-ons after pilot
